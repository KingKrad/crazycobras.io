PRIVACY

Without an account

Your garage, cash, best score and settings are saved in this browser (with a backup copy, see below). When you type an answer that isn't on the game's built-in list, the answer text and the question are sent to our server, which checks it against our list of approved answers and looks it up on Wikidata. Wikidata sees only the question and the answer, sent from our server, never your IP address, name or account, so the answer isn't linked to you. Results are cached for a day.

With an account

An account is needed only to buy Unlimited, and accounts are for players 13 and over. We store your email address, salted hashes of your password and recovery code (never the password or code themselves), whether you have Unlimited, the ID of your Stripe checkout, and the list of prompts you have already raced (so they don't repeat on your other devices; without an account that list stays on your device). If you finish a daily race while logged in, its distance goes on that day's public leaderboard under your leaderboard name (a nickname you choose, or "RACER" and four letters; never your email), and we keep your daily-race streak and how many Unlimited races you've run. A login cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days. If you sign in with Google or Apple, they tell us only your email address (with Apple, it can be a private relay address) and an ID for your account with them; we never see your Google or Apple password, and such an account has no password with us.

Your game progress (cash, cars, passport stamps and stats) is saved in your browser, with or without an account. So it isn't lost if your browser clears it (iPhone Safari does after 7 days away), we also keep a backup copy on our server under a random device ID stored in a cookie. That backup contains only game progress, nothing that identifies you, and only that browser can get it back. When you're logged in, a copy of your game progress is also kept on your account, so it follows you to any device you log in on; it's deleted with your account.

To improve the game we count, without any player ID, which prompts are played, how often they're answered at each rarity tier or missed, and how often each accepted answer is given (and, to find good answers missing from our lists, how often each answer that didn't count is tried, also without any player ID, with anything but letters and numbers removed). If you use "Report" on the results screen, we keep the prompt, the answer, the reason and anything you write in the note; if you're logged in it's linked to your account. For accounts we also note when you last opened the game and when you bought Unlimited.

Online 1v1 races keep, for two hours, the names the two players typed, their cars, the race settings, each round's answer and rarity, and the list of prompts the joining player has already raced (sent to the host's game so the race picks prompts new to both of you), so both games can show the race; then they're deleted.

Payments (Unlimited and premium cars) are handled by Stripe, and we keep which premium cars your account has bought. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; Stripe keeps its own record of the payment under its privacy policy.

The sign-up form may show a Cloudflare Turnstile check, which looks at your browser to tell people from bots, without cookies for ads or tracking. Our hosts, Vercel (the website) and Cloudflare (accounts and answer checks), briefly log requests to the site (including IP addresses) to run it and to stop abuse such as password guessing. To limit log-in and payment attempts, we also count them for a few minutes per IP address and per email, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

We count how many races are started each day, by kind (solo or 2P, and mode), with nothing about who played. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics trackers.

Deleting your account

Go to ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT in the game and confirm with your password (or, for a Google or Apple account, by typing DELETE). That removes your email, password hash, login sessions, the copy of your progress and prompt list kept on the account, and your leaderboard scores. Unlimited is lost with the account. Stripe's record of a past payment stays with Stripe.

Questions

Questions about your data, or a request to see or delete it: email support@vroomillion.com (you can also delete your account yourself on the Account screen).
