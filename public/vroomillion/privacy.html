PRIVACY

Without an account

Your garage, cash, best score and settings are saved only in this browser. Nothing about your games is sent anywhere.

With an account

An account is needed only to buy Unlimited, and accounts are for players 13 and over. We store your email address, salted hashes of your password and recovery code (never the password or code themselves), whether you have Unlimited, and the ID of your Stripe checkout. A login cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; Stripe keeps its own record of the payment under its privacy policy.

Our host, Cloudflare, briefly logs requests to the site (including IP addresses) to run it and to stop abuse such as password guessing.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics trackers.

Deleting your account

Go to ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT in the game and confirm with your password. That removes your email, password hash and login sessions. Unlimited is lost with the account. Stripe's record of a past payment stays with Stripe.
