PRIVACY

Without an account

Your garage, cash, best score and settings are saved only in this browser. When you type an answer that isn't on the game's built-in list, the answer text and the question are sent to our server, which looks it up on Wikidata to see if it counts. Wikidata sees the request as coming from our server, not from you, and the answer isn't linked to you or your account. Lookups are cached for a day.

With an account

An account is needed only to buy Unlimited, and accounts are for players 13 and over. We store your email address, salted hashes of your password and recovery code (never the password or code themselves), whether you have Unlimited, the ID of your Stripe checkout, and the list of prompts you have already raced (so they don't repeat on your other devices; without an account that list stays on your device). If you finish a daily race while logged in, its distance goes on that day's public leaderboard under your leaderboard name (a nickname you choose, or "RACER" and four letters; never your email), and we keep your daily-race streak and how many Unlimited races you've run. A login cookie keeps you signed in for 30 days.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us; Stripe keeps its own record of the payment under its privacy policy.

The sign-up form may show a Cloudflare Turnstile check, which looks at your browser to tell people from bots, without cookies for ads or tracking. Our host, Cloudflare, briefly logs requests to the site (including IP addresses) to run it and to stop abuse such as password guessing. To limit log-in and payment attempts, we also count them for a few minutes per IP address and per email, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.

We count how many races are started each day, by kind (solo or 2P, and mode), with nothing about who played. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics trackers.

Deleting your account

Go to ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT in the game and confirm with your password. That removes your email, password hash and login sessions. Unlimited is lost with the account. Stripe's record of a past payment stays with Stripe.
