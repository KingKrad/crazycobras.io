HOW TO PLAY

  1. Every race is 7 questions. Each one has lots of right answers.
  2. Type one. The more niche your answer, the further your car blasts down the road.
  3. Almost anything real counts. If your answer isn't on our list, we check it against Wikipedia's database while the clock runs — so Mayochup counts as a condiment. Answers found that way are rare, so they score high.
  4. Made a small typo? We show the fix in the box — press GO again to use it, or type something else. Not on the list? Try again — keep guessing until the timer runs out.
  5. Your distance is your score, and your car multiplies it: a beater ×1, the best hypercars ×5. Every destination also needs a car class (the Moon needs Class S), so upgrade to go further. Higher classes earn more cash per metre too.
  6. Daily races: two free races a day, the same prompts for everyone. Unlimited adds numbered races (RACE #1, #2...) in every mode.
  7. No repeats: once you've seen a prompt it never comes back, unless you hit REDO THIS RACE on the results screen to replay the same prompts. CLASSIC has over 400 broad prompts; letter twists like starting with "M" are in HARD MODE.
  8. Typos: a close misspelling is fixed for you (press Enter again to use it) and scores the same as the real answer, One in a Vroomillion included.
  9. Destinations: your total distance at the end of a race decides where you end up — from the scrapyard to the Moon. Each new place gets a passport stamp. There's an exclusive area at the very top, and getting there unlocks a car you can't buy.
  10. Cartoon shows: real people who appeared count too — Donald Trump is a South Park character. The more famous the cameo, the less it scores.
  11. CLASSIC: sports, TV and movies, music and lots of geography, plus food, animals and more.
  12. GEARHEAD: every question is about cars and how they work: engines, brakes, warning lights, models and makers, with the odd racing one. Pick it on the race setup screen.
  13. HARD MODE: the niche stuff: one band's songs, one show's characters, typefaces, knots, chess openings and more.
  14. 2P: same answer as your friend? FENDER BENDER — you both drop a tier.
  15. Hit NITRO before you answer for 1.5× distance. Charges depend on your car.
  16. Free: the 2 daily races. Unlimited ($5, once) unlocks as many races as you like, plus 2P, GEARHEAD and HARD MODE.
