HOW TO PLAY

THE BASICS

  • Every race is 7 prompts, each with lots of right answers. Type one: the more niche it is, the further your car blasts down the road.
  • Almost anything real counts. Not on our list? We check it while the clock runs (Wikipedia, or our AI referee), so Mayochup counts as a condiment. Wrong? Keep guessing until the timer runs out.
  • Small typo? We show the fix in the box; press GO again to use it. It scores the same as the real answer.
  • Your car multiplies your distance (a beater ×1, the best hypercars ×5). Hit NITRO before you answer for 1.5×. Race a car to level it up (more power, nitro and new paint) and collect them all in the GARAGE: new deals every day.
  • Your total distance takes you somewhere, from the scrapyard to the Moon. Each new place is a passport stamp, and the further ones need a better car class.
  • No repeats: you never see a prompt twice, in any mode, until you've played them all.

MODES & RULES

  • Daily race: free, once a day, the same seven prompts for everyone, with a leaderboard.
  • Unlimited ($5, once): as many races as you like, plus 2P, ONLINE 1V1, GEARHEAD (cars and how they work) and HARD MODE (niche stuff).
  • 2P and online 1v1: same prompts for both of you. Same answer? FENDER BENDER: a coin toss decides who keeps the points, and the loser gets half. Online, the host sends a code or link and the friend joins free.
  • CRAZY MODE flips the scoring: the more obvious, the further you go, and only the 2 most obvious answers to each prompt hit the JACKPOT (just for fun: no best runs or stamps). EQUAL CARS makes cars not matter in a 1v1.
  • REDO THIS RACE on the results screen replays the same prompts.
