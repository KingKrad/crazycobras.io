Playing for free

Anyone can play the daily race (one solo race a day, the same prompts for everyone) for free on each device, no account needed, and join a friend's online 1v1 race for free. Your progress, and whether you've played today's free race, are saved in your browser.

Unlimited

Unlimited costs $5 USD, paid once. It is not a subscription and you will not be charged again. It removes the daily limit and unlocks as many races as you like, 2P VERSUS, creating ONLINE 1V1 races, GEARHEAD and HARD MODE on your VROOMILLION account, on any device where you log in. Payments are handled by Stripe.

No refunds

All sales are final. Unlimited starts the moment you pay, so it can't be returned or refunded. Before paying you're asked to confirm that you understand this. This doesn't take away any rights the law where you live gives you that can't be waived.

Getting help

Email support@vroomillion.com for help with your account, a purchase, a refund question, a wrong answer in the game, or anything else.

Your account

Accounts are for players 13 and over. One account per person. Keep your password and recovery code safe: the recovery code is the only way to reset a forgotten password, and we can't look up a lost one.

Don't cheat with bots or scripts, attack the site, or share an account to get around the free limit. We may close accounts that do, without a refund.

The game

Trivia answers come from the game's makers and from Wikidata (free to reuse under CC0). An answer that isn't on our lists may be checked on Wikidata or judged by an AI referee. How rare an answer is was judged by the makers, worked out from how widely it's covered on Wikipedia, or estimated by the AI referee, not measured from what players type, so it will sometimes be wrong. Questions, cars, prices for in-game cash and features may change. If VROOMILLION ever shuts down, Unlimited ends with it.

The game is provided as it is, without guarantees that it will always be available or error-free.

VROOMILLION is a fan-made game. It is not affiliated with any car maker or with Krillion. Car brands that appear as trivia answers belong to their owners and are mentioned only as facts.
