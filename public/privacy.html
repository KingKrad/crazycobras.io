Privacy

RoomRemix doesn't create accounts, and it doesn't store your photos or descriptions.

Anything you upload is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to make one image and a shopping list, and then it is discarded.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you're human.

Shopping links go to outside stores, which have their own privacy policies.

AI images are concept art, not construction plans. Check with a professional before any structural, electrical or plumbing work.
