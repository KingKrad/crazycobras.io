How it works

1. Learn in bite-size lessons

Each lesson takes about 10 minutes: read a little, change your game, answer a quick question. Earn XP and keep your streak going.

2. Your game updates live

Next to every lesson is a real daily puzzle game. Rename it, re-theme it, tune it, even write code for it — and play it instantly.

3. Build with AI

You’ll learn to ask AI helpers for exactly the code you need, paste it in, and fix errors by feeding them back. No coding experience needed.

4. Launch

In the last lesson you download your finished game as a single file, put it online for free, and share it. Then submit it to our showcase.

Price

Unit 1 (3 lessons) is free with no sign-up. The full course is a one-time $19 — no subscription.

Honest note

We teach you to build and launch a game. We can’t promise it will go viral or make money — nobody honestly can.
