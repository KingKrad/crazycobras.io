Privacy

Last updated: November 2026. Template text — have it reviewed before launch.

Without an account

Your progress, game settings and preferences are stored only in your browser (localStorage). We don’t use tracking cookies or ads.

With an account

We store your email address, your course progress and your game settings so they sync across devices, and a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your email and payment status, never your card details.

Email

We only email you sign-in links you asked for.

Showcase

If you submit a game, its name, description and link are shown publicly once approved. Your email is never shown.

Your rights

Ask us to delete your account and data at any time using the contact on your receipt.
